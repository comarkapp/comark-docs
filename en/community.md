# Community

Welcome to the Copicseal community. You're invited to join the group chats, share tips, and talk about workflows with other users.

Before reporting an issue or requesting a feature, please follow this order:

1. Check the FAQ or search existing [issues](https://github.com/copicseal/copicseal/issues).
2. If the problem is still unresolved, open a new [issue](https://github.com/copicseal/copicseal/issues/new) with as much detail as possible.
